Voxa — PDF & Text to Audio

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how Voxa — PDF & Text to Audio handles information when you use the Android application.

1. Data Controller and Contact

Voxa is operated by Josue Giovanni Valdivia Urrutia, an independent developer.

For privacy-related questions, contact voxa.app.support@gmail.com.

2. How Voxa Works

Voxa converts text and PDF documents containing selectable text into audio. It also lets you listen to the result and export audio files to a location you choose.

The application does not require a user account and does not use its own server to process text or generate audio with the integrated natural voice model.

Text is processed on your device. Voxa does not maintain a permanent history of the text you enter. PDF documents are used to extract the text needed for conversion; the application does not provide its own cloud-based document library.

Voices provided by Android are system services and may require an Internet connection, depending on the voice and your device settings.

3. Audio Files and Documents

When exporting audio, you choose where to save it. The file is subject to the storage, backup, and deletion options of Android and the location you select.

Voxa does not provide its own cloud storage service. If you share a file through another application, subsequent handling of that file is governed by the receiving application or service.

4. Preferences and Data Stored on Your Device

Voxa stores preferences needed for its operation, such as certain usage settings and acceptance of the terms. Google UMP may also retain information related to your advertising consent choices.

Depending on your device and Android settings, certain application data may be included in system backups. Uninstalling the application does not guarantee that exported files stored elsewhere will be deleted.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Voxa uses Google AdMob to display advertisements and Google's consent platform (UMP) to manage advertising privacy choices where applicable.

Google's advertising components may collect and share technical and usage data, such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, interactions with the application and advertisements, and diagnostic information. This data may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, and maintain advertising services.

The specific processing depends on the SDK version, advertising configuration, region, and your consent choices. Advertising requests are made according to the consent status and the options allowed by the integrated configuration.

You can read Google's Privacy Policy and its information about AdMob privacy and consent.

6. Permissions and Internet Access

The application uses Android features needed to process files, play audio, save and share results. It also uses Internet access for advertising services and for system voices that require it.

Voxa does not provide user accounts, its own advertising tracking, or its own analytics service. Third-party providers may process information in connection with the services they provide, as described in this policy.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Text you enter is not retained as a permanent reading history within Voxa. Exported files remain where you save them until you delete them. Preferences and consent data may remain on your device for as long as they are needed for the application or integrated services.

Data received by Google or other providers is retained according to their applicable policies and settings. To request information about Google's data processing, consult its privacy resources.

8. Security

Voxa is designed to process text and generate natural voice audio primarily on your device, without sending that content to its own server. Data transmitted through third-party services is subject to those providers' security measures and practices.

No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Voxa is intended for people aged 13 and older. It is not specifically designed for children under that age. Applicable restrictions and obligations may vary by country and by the configuration of advertising services.

10. Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access, correction, updating, deletion, or other actions regarding your personal data, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make an inquiry, contact voxa.app.support@gmail.com. Requests will be assessed according to applicable law and the type of data involved. Requests concerning data processed directly by Google or other providers may need to be directed to those providers.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if Voxa's features, integrated services, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the beginning identifies when this version takes effect. If material changes are made, the published policy will be updated.

12. Contact

If you have questions about Voxa or this Privacy Policy, contact voxa.app.support@gmail.com.
